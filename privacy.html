Zipline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Zipline ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Furkan Doganay. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — solved puzzles, times, streaks, settings and theme choice — is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active Zipline Pro subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information (such as OS version and country). RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
